### Weeks 9-12: Continuing to Learn AWS After the Internship

With the CloudHop RAG project and the official 8-week worklog period now complete, weeks 9 through 12 are set aside to keep building on what the internship started rather than letting that momentum stop. The plan for this stretch is to go deeper on AWS services the project only touched briefly - S3 Metadata/Annotations, Glue, Cognito, and Cost Categories - work through AWS Skill Builder learning paths and practice exams toward the AWS Certified Solutions Architect - Associate certification, and periodically check in on the deployed CloudHop RAG stack to keep Free Tier usage healthy and costs in line with the estimate in chapter 5.13. It is also the time to act on the two improvement areas flagged in the self-evaluation - spacing work out more evenly instead of finishing close to deadlines, and communicating progress more concisely - by applying them to self-directed study instead of a mentor-assigned schedule. The intent is not to start a new large project, but to turn four more weeks of independent practice into a habit of continuous AWS learning that carries on well past the internship itself.
